# iSputnik TV — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

iSputnik TV is a television app for **iSputnik**, a media library that a household runs on its
own server. The app has no service of its own behind it: it shows what is on the server the
person connects it to, and nothing else.

## What the app keeps on the TV

- **The address of the server** typed into Connect.
- **A sign-in token** from that server, given when someone approves the TV with Link a device.
  Unlinking the TV from its menu, or removing it on the server under Profile › Devices, ends it.
- **Small preferences**: the chosen viewing profile, subtitle and audio choices, and similar
  settings.

These stay on the TV. The app turns off Android backup, so they are not copied anywhere.

## Who the app talks to

- **Only the server the person typed in.** Browsing, playback, photos, games, the speed test,
  watched marks and profile changes are all requests to that server, under that household's
  own rules. What the server records about them is up to the household that runs it.
- The app built for Amazon Appstore and Google Play makes no other connections. (The edition
  downloaded from GitHub also asks GitHub whether a newer version exists.)

## What the developer receives

Nothing. The app contains no analytics, advertising, crash reporting or tracking of any kind,
and sends no data to the developer or to any third party. We cannot see who uses the app,
which servers it connects to, or what is watched.

## Children

The app is not directed at children. A household may set up a Kids profile on its own server;
that is a filter the household controls, and it sends nothing to us either.
